I design optical measurements and the models that learn from them, for imaging and for perception without images. Long term, I want AI systems and embodied robots to genuinely understand the physical world through what they sense.

- **Now:** acquisition-aware learning and pretrained multimodal models for bucket-detector signals; time-domain diffuse optics during my visit to Politecnico di Milano.
- **Open to:** research collaborations and research internships.
